# E01 cross-platform decisions

Product owner decisions on 2026-10-06: tvOS-first reference, webOS 22+, sideload then LG Store, TV-only (no additional external server), repository-only visual references, and authorized AerioTV branding/source reuse. The entries here apply those decisions to concrete **pinned-source** differences; they are not hardware-capability findings. E02 owns outstanding viability tests.

**Later scope update:** The owner deferred multiview without a target date.
The E01 inventory still specifies upstream tiles/menus for a later E10 phase;
the current TV app hides multiview entry points and settings. E02.3 does not
gate the current feasibility epic or release. See
[E02 evidence](webos-feasibility.md) for LG's documented media limit.

### Decisions requiring measurement, not speculation

E02.1: authenticated media requests/CORS and LAN; E02.2: decoding, tracks, subtitles, speed and diagnostic access; E02.3: multiview stream count/composition; E02.4: local recording, download, time-shift and background reminders; E02.5: sync/companion/home integration and LG Store permissions. A capability failure is a documented parity gap requiring product-owner acceptance, not a silent scope cut. No unapproved external compute service may be introduced.

### Baseline change control

Treat the two pinned commits as a snapshot, not a promise to follow all future upstream changes. Any deliberate rebaseline must record which menus/behaviors changed, amend the relevant Beads issue and reference links, and obtain product approval for changed scope. Earlier `docs/SettingsUIRedesign.md` is historical: at the pinned Apple snapshot, settings rail and two-pane host **already ship** and App Behaviors/Network/Multiview settings were regrouped under Live TV/Player/General; follow current `Features/Settings/SettingsView.swift:1021-1110`.
